# Niche Energy: Automotive

## Energy
Industrial bold. This is the most aggressive dial alongside fitness — automotive shops earn trust through raw competence, not politeness. Lean into mechanical, high-torque confidence: hard edges, dense blacks, one screaming accent. Nothing here should feel soft, rounded, or apologetic. If a section looks like it could belong to a dentist, it's wrong.

## Palette direction
- **Shop floor**: `bg-zinc-950` base, off-white body copy (`text-zinc-100`), single accent `#ea580c` (signal orange) used at full commitment — full-width CTA bands, oversized numerals, border rules.
- **Signal red**: `bg-neutral-950` base with `#dc2626` accent for performance/racing-leaning shops — accent on stat callouts and hover states, never diluted into a gradient.
- **Acid variant**: `bg-zinc-950` with acid yellow `#eab308` accent for tire/quick-service shops wanting a pit-lane feel; pair with `text-zinc-400` for secondary copy.
Commit to exactly one accent per site. Never mix orange and red on the same page.

## Type direction
`font-tech` uppercase for all display type — hero and section headings set `uppercase tracking-[-0.02em]` with `font-bold`. Body copy drops to `font-sans` sentence case for readability. Numerals (prices, turnaround times, years in business) get oversized treatment in `font-tech`, e.g. `text-[clamp(4rem,10vw,8rem)]`.

## Photo treatment
Full-bleed only — no framed or card-cropped shop photos. Machinery, tools, and vehicles fill the section edge-to-edge with `object-cover`. Apply a dark overlay gradient (`from-black/80 to-black/20`) whenever text sits over an image so the accent color still reads as the loudest thing on the page. Avoid stock imagery that looks like a dealership brochure — favor tight, grease-and-metal detail shots over wide showroom shots.

## Copy attitude
Short, declarative, verb-first. No "Welcome to" or "Quality service you can trust" filler — say what happens and how fast.
- "Brakes that bite."
- "Your car, out by 5."
- "We fix what the dealer won't."
