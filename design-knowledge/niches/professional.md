# Niche Energy: Professional

## Energy
Structured bold — the fallback dial for real estate, legal, financial, and other authority-driven services with no more specific niche match. Confidence here comes from order and precision: grids, numbers, and restraint communicate competence. Bold through structure, not noise — one strong accent, disciplined layout, no decorative flourish.

## Palette direction
- **Navy authority**: `bg-[#0f1f3d]` navy dark sections paired with `bg-white` light sections, one warm accent `#c08a3e` (muted gold/brass) used on rules, kickers, and the primary CTA only.
- **Charcoal precision**: `bg-zinc-900` charcoal base with a warm terracotta accent `#b5502e` for a less corporate, more boutique-firm feel.
- **Editorial navy**: `bg-white` base with navy `#1e3a5f` for headlines and a single warm accent (`#c08a3e`) reserved for stat numerals and CTA — everything else stays black/white/navy for a legal-brief precision.
Accent appears exactly once per section maximum — on a rule, a numeral, or a CTA — never as a background wash; the restraint itself signals authority.

## Type direction
`font-heading` sentence case, `font-semibold` weight — confident but not shouting. Tight tracking (`tracking-[-0.03em]`) on headlines. Favor editorial-list layouts: numbered service lists, stat grids with oversized numerals in the accent color, and tabular data treatments over illustrative graphics.

## Photo treatment
Restrained imagery — one or two high-quality environmental portraits (agent headshot, office, courtroom-adjacent architecture) rather than photo-heavy sections. When used, frame rather than full-bleed (`rounded-xl` with a border), signaling composure. Prefer data visualizations, stat bands, and numbered lists over photography as the primary visual driver — the numbers and structure ARE the imagery.

## Copy attitude
Authority through specificity — cite real numbers and outcomes, avoid vague trust claims.
- "Sold in 21 days, average."
- "Won on appeal, twice."
- "Your case, not a caseload."
