# Niche Energy: Trades

## Energy
Bold utility. Plumbers, electricians, HVAC, and general contractors sell speed and reliability under pressure — the site should read like a work van pulling up fast, not a corporate brochure. Confident, no-nonsense, slightly urgent. Every section should reinforce "we show up and we fix it."

## Palette direction
- **Site-safety**: `bg-[#0b3954]` (deep blue) base with safety-orange accent `#f97316` on CTAs, stat bands, and border rules — the classic hi-vis pairing read instantly as "tradesperson," not generic corporate blue.
- **Warm neutral**: `bg-zinc-50` light sections paired with `bg-[#0b3954]` dark color-block sections, orange accent carried through both for continuity.
- **High-visibility**: safety orange `#f97316` as the dominant color-block background in at least one full-bleed section, deep blue reserved for text and secondary elements.
Repeat the CTA color relentlessly — trades customers scan fast, looking for the "call now" button; the accent should never be subtle.

## Type direction
`font-heading` for all display type, set in heavy weight (`font-bold` or `font-black`), sentence case (not uppercase — trades copy should feel spoken, not shouted like automotive). Tight tracking (`tracking-[-0.02em]`) on headlines, generous line-height on body copy for fast scanning by someone on their phone with a burst pipe.

## Photo treatment
Full-bleed action shots — technician mid-repair, van on the street, before/after job photos. Avoid posed stock photography of people in hard hats shaking hands. When text overlays an image, use a solid dark overlay (`bg-black/60`) rather than a gradient, so emergency CTAs stay legible at a glance. Before/after pairs work well as a split-image treatment.

## Copy attitude
Blunt, outcome-first, time-bound. Answer "how fast" and "how much" before anything else.
- "Burst pipe? Gone by lunch."
- "Same-day service. No overtime charge."
- "Licensed, insured, actually on time."
