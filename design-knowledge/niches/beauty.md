# Niche Energy: Beauty

## Energy
High-fashion editorial. Salons, spas, and beauty studios sell transformation and taste — the site should feel like a fashion editorial spread, not a service menu. Confidence comes from whitespace and typographic scale, not noise. This is bold through minimalism: fewer elements, each one considered.

## Palette direction
- **Cream & blush**: `bg-[#faf5f2]` cream base, blush accent `#e8b4b8` on detail chips and hover underlines, near-black `#0a0a0a` for display type — high contrast, low saturation.
- **Black & bone**: `bg-zinc-950` full-bleed dark sections against `bg-[#f5f1ea]` bone light sections, no accent color at all beyond the value contrast itself — pure editorial restraint.
- **Blush statement**: blush `#e8b4b8` as a full-bleed color-block section background (not just an accent), with black serif type set directly on top for a fashion-plate moment.
Whitespace is the real "accent" here — resist the urge to fill every section; generous negative space signals taste.

## Type direction
`font-elegant` serif, oversized, with tight leading (`leading-[0.92]` to `leading-[0.98]`) and tight tracking (`tracking-[-0.03em]`). Sentence case, never uppercase — uppercase serif reads bridal-invitation, not editorial. Hero display sizes should push the upper end of the scale (`text-[clamp(3.5rem,9vw,8rem)]`).

## Photo treatment
Editorial photo crops in portrait aspect ratio (`aspect-[3/4]` or taller), never square thumbnails. Full-bleed portrait hero shots or asymmetric grid placements with generous surrounding whitespace. Minimal or no overlay — let the photo's own contrast carry the mood; if text must overlay, use a subtle `bg-black/30` at most. Avoid busy multi-photo grids; one or two considered images outperform a gallery wall.

## Copy attitude
Minimal, confident, declarative — often just a few words per line, deliberately broken.
- "Hair that turns heads."
- "Your best face, forward."
- "Not a trim. A transformation."
