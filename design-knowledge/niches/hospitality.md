# Niche Energy: Hospitality

## Energy
Warm editorial. Cafes, restaurants, bars, and small hotels sell atmosphere and craft, not urgency. The site should feel unhurried — like a well-shot magazine spread about the place, not a menu PDF. Confidence here comes from restraint and sensory detail, not scale of type.

## Palette direction
- **Stone & cream**: `bg-stone-950` dark sections paired with `bg-[#faf6f0]` cream light sections, amber accent `#b45309` used sparingly on rules, kickers, and hover states.
- **Terracotta warmth**: `bg-[#fdf8f3]` base with terracotta accent `#c2603d` for CTAs and detail chips — reads as artisanal, not corporate.
- **Deep night**: `bg-stone-950` full-bleed for evening/bar-forward concepts, warm amber `#d97706` accent glowing against the dark like candlelight.
Accent should feel like a garnish, not a highlighter — used on kickers, thin rules, and one CTA, never as a loud color-block background.

## Type direction
`font-elegant` serif display in sentence case for all headlines — never uppercase, never shouting. Tight but readable leading (`leading-[1.05]` to `leading-[1.1]`). Body copy in `font-sans` for contrast against the serif display. Kickers (location, est. year) in small tracked-out caps: `text-sm tracking-[0.2em] uppercase`.

## Photo treatment
Food and interior photography dominant — full-bleed hero images and split-editorial layouts (text column beside a tall image column) both work well. Photos should feel shot, not stock: warm color grading, shallow depth of field, natural light. Avoid harsh overlays; if text sits over an image, use a soft gradient (`from-black/60 to-transparent`) that preserves the photo's warmth rather than flattening it.

## Copy attitude
Sensory, unhurried, specific. Evoke a feeling or a moment rather than listing features.
- "Slow mornings. Strong coffee."
- "Dinner, the way it used to be."
- "Come for the wine list. Stay for the light."
