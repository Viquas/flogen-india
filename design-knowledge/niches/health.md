# Niche Energy: Health

## Energy
Confident clean-bold — the most restrained dial in the library. Dentists, clinics, physio, and allied health sell trust and calm competence; a patient who is anxious should feel reassured, not overwhelmed. Still bold (never timid or generic-corporate), but the boldness is a single deliberate move per page, not a barrage. No uppercase shouting, no aggressive color blocking.

## Palette direction
- **Clinical calm**: `bg-white` base with deep-green accent `#0f5132` used as one bold move — a full-width stat band or a color-block CTA section — plus `text-zinc-900` for headlines.
- **Trust navy**: `bg-white` base, navy `#1e3a5f` accent on the single bold section (booking CTA band or trust-marker strip), rest of the page stays white/light-gray (`bg-zinc-50`) for cleanliness.
- **Warm reassurance**: `bg-[#f7f5f0]` warm-white base with deep-green `#0f5132` accent restricted to icons, underlines, and one CTA button — no full-bleed color sections at all if the practice skews pediatric or anxiety-sensitive.
Pick exactly ONE bold move (a stat band, a color-block CTA, or an oversized trust number) — do not stack multiple loud moves on one page.

## Type direction
`font-heading` in sentence case throughout — never uppercase. Weight can go to `font-semibold` or `font-bold` for headlines to keep the "still bold" requirement, but tracking stays closer to neutral (`tracking-[-0.02em]`, not the aggressive `-0.04em` used elsewhere) so it reads composed rather than urgent. Body copy comfortably sized (`text-lg`) for older or anxious readers.

## Photo treatment
Framed, warm, human-centered photography — patient-practitioner interaction, clean interiors, natural light. Avoid full-bleed clinical/sterile imagery that reads cold. When photos anchor a section, pair with rounded corners (`rounded-2xl`) rather than hard edges, reinforcing approachability. Trust markers (certifications, review counts, years in practice) should sit prominently near the fold, not buried in a footer.

## Copy attitude
Warm, plain-spoken, reassuring — address the fear directly, then resolve it.
- "Dentistry without the dread."
- "Same-day appointments, real answers."
- "Care that actually listens."
