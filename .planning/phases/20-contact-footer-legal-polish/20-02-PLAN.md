**5. Create components/marketing/cookie-consent.tsx:**

"use client" component. Minimal banner at bottom of viewport.

Logic:
- On mount: check `localStorage.getItem('cookie-consent')`. If value exists ('accepted' or 'declined'), do NOT render banner.
- If no preference stored: render banner.
- Accept button: `localStorage.setItem('cookie-consent', 'accepted')`, hide banner.
- Decline button: `localStorage.setItem('cookie-consent', 'declined')`, hide banner.
- Use `useState<boolean | null>(null)` initialized to null. In useEffect, check localStorage and set to true (show) or false (hide). This avoids hydration mismatch since localStorage is client-only.

Styling:
- `fixed bottom-0 left-0 right-0 z-50` — above everything
- Dark background with slight transparency: `bg-[#0A0A0A]/95 backdrop-blur-sm`
- Border-top: `border-t border-[var(--mkt-border)]`
- Flex row: text on left, two buttons on right
- Text: "We use cookies to improve your experience." in muted color
- Accept button: accent bg (#AF92FF), dark text, rounded
- Decline button: ghost style, border only
- Responsive: stack vertically on mobile (flex-col sm:flex-row)
- Smooth enter animation: translate-y-full to translate-y-0 transition on mount
